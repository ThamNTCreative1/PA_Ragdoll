TUNG SAHUR MINECRAFT IDLE

TungSahur_Minecraft_Idle.fbx / .glb
540 triangles, 4 bones, 1 vertex-colour material, no image textures.
4-second loop; two gentle 1.8 cm dips. Feet fixed. Both arms swing gently
forward/back by 3 degrees in opposite phases; the bat stays with its hand.
Source and independently re-imported FBX and GLB checked at multiple frames:
zero non-manifold edges, foot drift and loop seam error.
