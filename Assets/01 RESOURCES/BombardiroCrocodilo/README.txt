BOMBARDIRO CROCODILO - APPROVED MODEL WITH IDLE
FINAL EXPORT - approved and exported on 2026-10-29.
Use Bombardiro_Idle.prefab for the animated character.
Final mesh checks: 145 closed components, zero inverted components, zero open
components. Imported Unity FBX: zero boundary edges, zero non-manifold edges,
zero degenerate triangles. Verified visually with backface culling from four
views, including the underside, after sampling the exported animation.

Minecraft/low-poly crocodile bomber rebuilt using the supplied reference's
silhouette: long jaws, individual white teeth, four propeller engines, wide
wings, rear stabilisers, upright tail and low crocodile dorsal scales.
2004 triangles; one mesh/material; vertex colours; no texture maps.
Normals validated: 145 closed components, zero inverted closed components,
zero zero-area faces. Geometry is separated into editable parts in Blender.
