LIRILI IDLE - REVIEW

Lirili_Idle_Preview.mp4: 4-second rendered preview.
Lirili_Idle.blend: editable source, active scene Lirili_Idle_Review.
Lirili_Idle.glb: one skinned mesh, one material, nine bones, one Idle clip.
Lirili_Idle.fbx: model, skeleton and animation for Unity's Generic importer.

Blender: press Space over the timeline/viewport to play the idle.
Playback uses frames 1-96 at 24 fps; frame 97 is the identical loop endpoint.
GLB animation time runs from 0 to 4 seconds.
The two sandals are bound to the stationary Root bone.
Body breathing, head sway, ear movement and a four-bone trunk chain are keyed.
There is no root translation or walking motion.

Validation on evaluated vertices:
Start/end loop seam: 0.0 metres.
Fixed-foot displacement: 0.0 metres.
Maximum displacement at frame 25: approximately 0.123 metres.
1680 triangles, one material, vertex colours, no texture maps.

Unity FBX import: Rig = Generic; enable Import Animation and Loop Time
for the Idle clip. Use a material supporting the exported vertex colours.
The animated asset has not replaced the earlier static Unity prefab.

Only Lirili is included in this review. Await user approval before the next model.
